    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Laramie, WY House

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Laramie

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Wyoming plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Laramie, WY

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Laramie

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Laramie, WY

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Some have self-cleaning dip tubes for sediment. Powered anodes last longest. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Laramie

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.     Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.
