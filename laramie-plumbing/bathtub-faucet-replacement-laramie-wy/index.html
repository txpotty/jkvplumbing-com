    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Laramie

    A water softener or filtration system is a common upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Cleaning helps lightly scaled valves, but pitted brass or a fused cartridge means replacing the faucet. We pull a cartridge to check.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Laramie

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Laramie

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Laramie

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. A handle that snapped off in the open position or a spout that broke loose from its pipe can leave water running or the tub unusable, so we treat those as urgent and send a plumber the same day when we can. Shut off the water first. Leave the tub drain open.
