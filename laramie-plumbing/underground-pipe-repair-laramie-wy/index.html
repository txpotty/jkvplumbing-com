    Pipe Bursting Services in Laramie, WY

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Laramie

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    HOA and Multifamily Underground Repair in Laramie

    HOA common areas and apartment complexes have long buried runs under parking and sidewalks. Tenants still need to get in.
