    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Laramie, WY

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Laramie

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Laramie

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Control Valve Repair in Laramie

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. JKV Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Parts for the common valves are stocked, which keeps most repairs to one visit.
