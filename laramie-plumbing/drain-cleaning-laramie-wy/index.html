    Jetting Versus Cabling in Laramie, WY

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Laramie

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Summer thunderstorms and hail can bring intense rain and flash flooding, especially in low areas and near drainages. We jet or cable the line to the outlet and pull the debris out of the catch basin, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.
