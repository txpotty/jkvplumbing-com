    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Laramie, WY

    High-hazard connections in Laramie, WY call for a reduced pressure zone assembly. Think chemical injection or medical equipment, where a backflow event could put something toxic in the supply, so the RPZ adds a relief valve between two checks that dumps water if either check leaks. We plan for the discharge.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Laramie

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. Two spring-loaded checks and four test cocks are inside, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. We size for flow.
