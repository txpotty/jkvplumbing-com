    Service Valve Kit Installs in Laramie, WY

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Laramie, WY

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. We can book it before you open or after you close.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Laramie

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Noritz Descaling in Laramie, WY

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. We bring common parts and a descaling kit on emergency calls, so most heaters are firing again before we pack up the truck.
