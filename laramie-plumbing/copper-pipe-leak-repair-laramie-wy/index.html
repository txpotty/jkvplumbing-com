    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Laramie

    A minor, accessible pinhole repair starts around $200. Hidden leaks cost more. Complex repairs like under-slab leaks can run past $2,000, with location, severity and footage of new pipe setting most of the price. You get a written number after locating.

    Clamps and epoxy putty can stop a drip for a day. They will not last. A proper repair means cutting out the thin copper and soldering or pressing in new pipe, and a DIY patch that lets go while you are at work can flood a room in the time it takes to drive home. Treat it as a stopgap.

    Minor repairs typically do not. Larger work often does. Significant repiping, relocating lines or work on the main water service in Laramie may need a plumbing permit, depending on the scope and how the city defines the work. We handle the filing.

    We typically use Type L copper. It is heavier than Type M. Type L lasts longer than the Type M found in many older homes, while press fittings or a PEX transition get used when a torch is a bad idea. Every part is lead-free.

    If you have had repeated leaks, it is worth pricing. PEX does not pinhole. PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and goes in quickly with fewer wall openings, so a PEX repipe often beats years of patching. You get both numbers.

    Keep pressure in range. Test it yearly. Soften hard water, limit recirculation pump run time and get any green crust on fittings looked at early, while homes with older copper should have it checked every few years. Small fixes now save drywall later.
