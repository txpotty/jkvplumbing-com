    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Laramie

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. JKV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Laramie, WY well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Laramie

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Laramie, WY

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Wyoming code when the job is done.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Laramie

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Laramie, WY

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.
