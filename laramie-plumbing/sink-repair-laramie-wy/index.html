    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Laramie, WY

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Laramie

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.
