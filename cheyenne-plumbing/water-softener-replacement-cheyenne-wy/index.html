    Fleck Valve Programming in Cheyenne, WY

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Cheyenne

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Cheyenne

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Cheyenne, WY

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. The valve's age decides it. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Softener Loop Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Cheyenne, WY

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Cheyenne

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Cheyenne, WY

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.
