    A plumbing inspection is a deeper look at one system. General inspectors check basic function. Home inspectors run water and flush toilets, and our plumbers add sewer video, pressure tests, pipe identification and code review.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. A damp wall, a musty cabinet or a warm spot on the floor can mean a hidden leak, and an inspection finds it before it spreads.

    Plan on one to two hours for most residential inspections. Big homes and long sewers add time.

    We check water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, the water heater, sump pump, garbage disposal and exposed gas lines. Drainage gets tested. Our plumber also checks for leaks, corrosion and vent issues, measures pressure and writes up the condition of every component.

    Watching your water meter, looking under sinks and checking for leaks are all things you can do yourself. A plumber adds video, pressure tests and an expert read.
