    Service Line Replacement Options in Cheyenne, WY

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Cheyenne

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, which is why pressure drops and the water runs orange after a shutoff, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Cheyenne

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Burst Main Line Response in Cheyenne, WY

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Cheyenne, WY

    Pipe pulling works like this. Two pits get dug, one at each end. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Frozen pipes are a common winter problem in Wyoming, and deep frost lines mean water service and sewer lines must be buried deep. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Cheyenne

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. Close the main shutoff at the house first. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will close the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Cheyenne, WY

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.
