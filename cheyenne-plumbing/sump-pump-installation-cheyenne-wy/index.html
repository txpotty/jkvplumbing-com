    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Cheyenne

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Cheyenne

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Cheyenne

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Cheyenne

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.
