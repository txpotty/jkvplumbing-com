    Sump Pump Maintenance in Cheyenne, WY: What We Check

    Pedestal Sump Pump Service in Cheyenne, WY

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. We check the rod for bends and the stops for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Cheyenne

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Cheyenne

    Drought is a recurring concern in Wyoming, and dry, shrinking soil can stress foundations and buried lines. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Cheyenne, WY

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. Tethered floats can wrap around the discharge pipe or snag on the pit wall. Vertical floats hang up once minerals coat the rod. For narrow basins where a float keeps catching, an electronic switch with no moving float is a good swap that ends the problem.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Cheyenne, WY

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Cheyenne, WY

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Float Switch Checks in Cheyenne, WY

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Next we adjust the on and off levels so each run is longer and the starts are fewer.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Cheyenne, WY

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.
