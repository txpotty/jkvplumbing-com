    Water Softener Repair in Cheyenne, WY

    A water softener or filtration system is a popular upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. The usual causes are a salt bridge in the brine tank or a plugged injector screen. JKV Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Slow Drain Repair in Cheyenne

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a blocked vent or a sagging drain line. We clear the line, camera it and repair the damaged section. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. No monthly snaking.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Cheyenne, WY

    Pinhole leaks in copper start from the inside, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. A water softener or filtration system is a common upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. One pinhole usually means others are coming in the same run.     Well Pump Repair in Cheyenne, WY

    No water at the tap on a well system narrows the list quickly. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. A submersible pull takes two techs and a truck.

    Sewer Line Repair in Cheyenne, WY

We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.
