    PEX Pipe Installation in Cheyenne

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. A water softener or filtration system is a popular upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    PEX Repiping in Cheyenne

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.
