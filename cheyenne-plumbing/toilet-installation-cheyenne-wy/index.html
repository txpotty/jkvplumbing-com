    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    Standard Toilet Installation in Cheyenne

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Cheyenne

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Cheyenne, WY

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Cheyenne

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Cheyenne, WY

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Cheyenne

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.
