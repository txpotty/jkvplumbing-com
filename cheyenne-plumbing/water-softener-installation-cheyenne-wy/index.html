    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Cheyenne

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Cheyenne

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff.     Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Cheyenne
