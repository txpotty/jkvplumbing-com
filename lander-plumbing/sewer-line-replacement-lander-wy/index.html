    How Pipe Bursting Works in Lander

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. Only two pits get dug. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Lander

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.
