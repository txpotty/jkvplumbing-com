    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Lander

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings.     Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Lander, WY

    Some homeowners in Lander, WY want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Lander

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. A water softener or filtration system is a popular upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
