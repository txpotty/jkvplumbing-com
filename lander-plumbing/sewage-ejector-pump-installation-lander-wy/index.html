    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Lander, WY

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements.     Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Lander, WY

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Redundancy matters here.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Lander

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. Venting still applies.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Lander

    Liberty ejector pumps show up on a lot of our installs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. A full-port valve gets added.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Lander

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Drought is a recurring concern in Wyoming, and dry, shrinking soil can shift foundations and buried lines. Backup power can help.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lander, WY

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lander

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Lander
