    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lander

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Lander

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter. New venting is part of it. Before quoting, we measure pipe runs and check the meter rating, which keeps the price from moving later.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lander, WY

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Lander, WY

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Lander

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lander

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Lander, WY

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Lander

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We set its air charge to match house pressure.
