    Softener Systems We Replace in Lander, WY

    Softener Loop Installation in Lander, WY

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Hose bibs stay off it. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Lander, WY

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. Outdoor hose bibs usually stay hard. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Lander, WY

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Obsolete valves are another story.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Lander

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Lander, WY

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Lander, WY

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Lander, WY

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Wyoming homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Lander, WY

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.
