    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Lander, WY

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Lander

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are common in parts of Wyoming, and their swelling and shrinking can crack foundations and shift buried pipes. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Lander, WY

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Lander, WY. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.
