    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Shut off the breaker to the pump. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, so we isolate the problem and get a new tank in, often the same day. Water back tonight.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Lander, WY

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Moisture eats steel tanks from the bottom. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Lander, WY

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Lander, WY

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. You get even pressure across fixtures. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Lander

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Commercial Well Pressure Tank Services in Lander, WY

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Some run four or more on a manifold. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.
