    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Lander

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Lander, WY

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. JKV Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Lander, WY

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Lander, WY

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Skipping the flush causes trouble. Every step gets done.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Lander

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.
