    What We Replace on Well Systems in Lander, WY

    Jet Pump Replacement in Lander, WY

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Lander, WY

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. You store water, not electricity. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Lander

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Lander

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. The soft start is easier on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Lander

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Well Pump Sizing in Lander

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Emergency Well Pump Replacement in Lander

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. We keep 1/2 and 3/4 HP submersibles on the truck. We also bring splice kits and switches.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Lander

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.
