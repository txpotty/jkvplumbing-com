    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Lander

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer.     Push-Button Drain Installation in Lander, WY

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Lander, WY

    Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up fast in water heaters, faucets and appliances.
