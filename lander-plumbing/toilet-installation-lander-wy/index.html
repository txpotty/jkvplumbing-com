    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Lander, WY

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Lander, WY

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Lander

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Lander, WY

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Lander, WY

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Lander, WY

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. Before setting fixtures in an office or shop, we check that the building supply can deliver the flow the valve is rated for, often 20 to 25 gallons per minute at the fixture. Fixture counts follow code.

PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Lander

    Setting a customer-supplied standard toilet usually costs $200 to $400 in labor. With a mid-range toilet included, most installs land between $450 and $900. Wall-hung systems with an in-wall carrier run $1,500 to $3,500 or more, mostly because the wall has to be opened, framed for the carrier and finished again afterward. We quote a flat price.

    Watch the base and the tank. A puddle that returns after wiping means a crack or a bad seal. A seal can be replaced for far less than a new toilet, so we check that first, but a crack in the china means replacement, since there is no lasting repair for cracked porcelain.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the toilet drain. Twelve inches is the most common. Some older homes and tight bathrooms have 10 or 14 inch rough-ins, and a toilet built for the wrong distance either will not fit against the wall or leaves an awkward gap behind the tank. Measure from the wall itself, not the baseboard.

    A one-piece toilet is cast as a single unit, so there is no tank-to-bowl joint to leak. Expect a higher price and more weight. Two-piece toilets join tank and bowl with a gasket, so they are easier to carry upstairs and you can replace one half if it cracks. Both flush well.

    We guarantee the workmanship on every toilet we set. A leak from our work gets fixed free. The seal and supply joint are on us, and factory defects go to the manufacturer, with our help on the claim. Hang on to your receipt.

    All the major ones, including Kohler, TOTO, American Standard and Gerber. Feel free to run a model past us first. We know which models flush well and which use odd parts that get hard to find once a flapper wears out.

    Homeowners set basic toilets all the time. The trouble spots are flange height and bolt torque. A low flange needs an extender ring, and one extra turn on a nut can split the base, which is why new floors and wall-hung units should go to a pro.
