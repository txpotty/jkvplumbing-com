    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water.     Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Green River

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Green River

    A water softener or filtration system is a common upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining.     Heat Exchanger Descaling in Green River

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger.     Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Green River
