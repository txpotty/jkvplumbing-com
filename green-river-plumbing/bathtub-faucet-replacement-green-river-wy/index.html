    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Green River

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Green River, WY

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Green River, WY

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Green River

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Green River, WY
