    Water Heater Maintenance in Green River

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up fast in water heaters, faucets and appliances. The tank gets flushed, the anode rod gets checked, the relief valve gets tested and the connections get inspected. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Green River, WY

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Green River

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. A yearly camera look and a cleaning on known problem lines, especially near big trees, keeps grease and roots from building to the point where the main backs up during a holiday. Jetting helps on grease. Root-prone lines go on a schedule.

    Water Softener Service in Green River

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Green River

    Manufactured homes in Wyoming need special attention to skirting and pipe insulation to prevent frozen supply and drain lines. Buried and under-slab water lines can wear from soil movement and hard water until they start leaking where nobody can see it, sometimes for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Green River

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Gas work is done to code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Sump Pump Testing in Green River, WY

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Drought is a recurring concern in Wyoming, and dry, shrinking soil can stress foundations and buried lines. Backup pumps get tested too.
