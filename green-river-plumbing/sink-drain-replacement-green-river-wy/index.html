    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Green River

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common long ago, and replacement brings them up to a vented P-trap. An AAV may be allowed where a vent is missing. Local rules decide.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Green River, WY

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Green River

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Green River, WY

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    P-Trap Replacement in Green River

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most.
