    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Rock Springs, WY

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Many rural homes in Wyoming draw from private wells, and well water can carry sulfate, iron or other minerals that affect taste and fixtures. Over years the opening shrinks. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Rock Springs, WY

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Rock Springs, WY

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Housing in Wyoming includes older ranch homes, manufactured homes and newer builds, with plumbing ranging from galvanized pipe to PEX. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.
