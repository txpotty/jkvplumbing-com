    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Rock Springs

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Rock Springs

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair.     Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Rock Springs

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Rock Springs

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. The footage shows each joint, crack and root intrusion. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, so we can find its exact spot and depth from the yard above, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Rock Springs, WY

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find.
