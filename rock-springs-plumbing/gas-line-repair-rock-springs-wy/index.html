    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Rock Springs, WY

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Rock Springs

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Rock Springs

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Rock Springs, WY

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Rock Springs, WY

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Rock Springs, WY
