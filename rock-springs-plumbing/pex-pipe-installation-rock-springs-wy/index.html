    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Rock Springs, WY

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Rock Springs

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Rock Springs

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
