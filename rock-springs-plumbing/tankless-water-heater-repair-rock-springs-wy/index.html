    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Springs, WY

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Rock Springs, WY

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, while a sensor or igniter repair tends to run $250 to $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor. A new unit costs a lot more. Installed, a new gas tankless typically runs $3,000 to $5,500, so on a unit past 15 years with a failed exchanger we usually lean toward replacing.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Rock Springs

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Most tankless installs include isolation valves with purge ports for exactly this job, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. Vinegar works in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Rock Springs

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, check for a crossover valve under the far sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Rock Springs, WY

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, and one burned element means weak heat at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rock Springs, WY

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Rock Springs

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Clear the code without flushing and it comes right back, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, circulate descaling solution through the exchanger for close to an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Rock Springs

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and it usually traces to scale or a backed-up condensate line. We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. We clean the trap and screen, then retest.
