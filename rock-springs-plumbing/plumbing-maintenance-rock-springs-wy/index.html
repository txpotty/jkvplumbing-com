    Hot Water Tank Service in Rock Springs, WY

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Rock Springs

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Rock Springs

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Resin cleaner helps. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Rock Springs

    Sewer problems hide underground. A camera run from the cleanout shows root intrusion, grease buildup, sags and offset joints long before they cause a backup into the lowest drain in the house.
