    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Rock Springs, WY

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Rock Springs

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Rock Springs

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Rock Springs

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Rock Springs

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.
