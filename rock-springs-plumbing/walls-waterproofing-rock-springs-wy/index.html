    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Rock Springs, WY

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Rock Springs

    Housing in Wyoming includes older ranch homes, manufactured homes and newer builds, with plumbing ranging from galvanized pipe to PEX. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Rock Springs

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Rock Springs

Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.
