    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Rock Springs, WY

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up fast in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.
