    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Rock Springs, WY

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Rock Springs

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Rock Springs

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Programming is the last step. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Rock Springs

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Rock Springs, WY

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Startup matters. We follow it step by step.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Rock Springs

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Rock Springs

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Rock Springs

    Most Rock Springs, WY softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. That typically covers the unit itself and the labor to plumb it in, program it and test it, with any loop or drain work listed separately. Bigger homes cost more. We give written pricing up front.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. An RO unit at the kitchen sink takes it back out.

    Ten to fifteen years is typical for a quality softener. Hardness, maintenance and build quality matter, and fresh resin can add years. Service helps.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. If pressure falls after the install, it usually points to an undersized valve or a clogged pre-filter, which our plumbers can fix.

    Standard residential softener installs often need no separate permit. Major main line or drain changes can need one, and we let you know. Permits are on us.

    Every one to two months is typical, depending on water use and hardness. Metered systems use salt more efficiently.

    Salt use depends on hardness, water use and how efficient the softener is. A family of four with fairly hard water might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month, and a metered high-efficiency model uses less. Metering cuts salt use.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale, but softeners work better on very hard water.
