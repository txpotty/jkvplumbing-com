    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Ranchettes

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set.     High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Ranchettes, WY
