    Humming Disposal Repair in Ranchettes

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Ranchettes, WY

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Fibrous scraps jam it most. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Ranchettes

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Ranchettes

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Check the trap arm if it jams again.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Ranchettes, WY

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. The choice is yours.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Ranchettes, WY

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. Common kits ride on our trucks. That keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Ranchettes, WY

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Ranchettes

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.
