    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Ranchettes

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Ranchettes

    Most home plumbing inspections run $150 to $350, depending on the size and age of the house. Sewer video is an add-on. A sewer camera adds to the total, and you hear the full price before we arrive, so the invoice matches the quote.

    Watching your water meter, looking under sinks and checking for leaks are all things you can do yourself. A professional inspection adds camera video, pressure testing and expert analysis.

    A one to two year routine works for most homes. Older homes and properties with past leaks may benefit from yearly inspections.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.
