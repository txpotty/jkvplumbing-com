    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Ranchettes

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Ranchettes

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down. Rocky ground can make digging for sewer or water line repairs difficult, and trenchless methods are sometimes a better option. It gets bedded in sand, not rocky backfill.     Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Ranchettes, WY

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Ranchettes, WY

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Ranchettes

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     Hydronic Heating with PEX in Ranchettes

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loops have length limits. Too long and the far end runs cold.
