    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Ranchettes

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs.     Listening Through Concrete in Ranchettes

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Ranchettes

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Ranchettes, WY

    A second slab leak is often a sign of more. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, ending the cycle of floor openings and surprise water bills. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Ranchettes

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Ranchettes

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Ranchettes, WY home.
