    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Ranchettes, WY

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas.     Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Ranchettes

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Rocky ground can make digging for sewer or water line repairs difficult, and trenchless methods are sometimes a better option. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Ranchettes, WY

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. Sizing is the first thing we check. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    CSST Repair in Ranchettes

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. We check bonding on every CSST job. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Ranchettes

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first.
