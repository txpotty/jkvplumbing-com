    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Ranchettes, WY

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Ranchettes, WY

    Rough-in sets the drain height and the stops long before the cabinets arrive.     Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Ranchettes, WY

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. Power is a separate question. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Ranchettes, WY

    A fireclay apron sink sits on a frame inside the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early.     Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Ranchettes

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Ranchettes

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.
