    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Sheridan, WY

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. We add up the BTU demand of everything on the line, measure the run, and size each branch so the furthest appliance still gets full pressure. New lines get pressure tested, always. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Sheridan

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. Each one is tested right after installation by a certified backflow tester, and the report goes to the water purveyor. That starts the annual clock. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    Sump Pump Installation in Sheridan, WY

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Discharge placement matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Sheridan

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. We size before we sell. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    Softener Loop and Conditioner Installs in Sheridan

    Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up fast in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Some homes already have a softener loop plumbed in, usually in the garage, which turns the install into a two-hour job. Without a loop, we tie into the main and build one. Figure on a few extra hours for that. A salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner fits the same loop.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Sheridan, WY

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded. Without that bond, a lightning strike nearby can arc through the tubing and start a fire.

    Water Heater Replacement in Sheridan

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. We install 40 and 50 gallon tanks, plus 75 gallon units for big households, from Rheem and Bradford White, with new flex connectors, a relief line piped to code and a vent check on every job. An expansion tank check is part of every job. One visit covers most installs.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Sheridan

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.
