    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Sheridan, WY

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Sheridan

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Sheridan, WY

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Sheridan, WY

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Sheridan

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Sheridan, WY

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Sheridan

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Sheridan, WY

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts, longer motor life.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Sheridan

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.
