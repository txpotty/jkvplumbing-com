    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Sheridan

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Sheridan

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. If the bowl is the cracked part, the whole toilet gets replaced, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Sheridan, WY

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. Replacement means matching the vessel brand for parts later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Sheridan, WY

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Sheridan, WY

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. Multiple toilets can be swapped in a single trip. Tenants keep a working bathroom. We coordinate entry directly with them, so you do not have to be there.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Sheridan

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Sheridan, WY

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. Bowls are matched to the flush valve's gallons per flush, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Sheridan

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. If there is room, a grab bar beside it helps too, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.
