    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Sheridan

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Sheridan

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Sheridan, WY

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Sheridan, WY

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Wyoming homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Sheridan, WY

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. We compare brands on that basis.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Sheridan

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.
