    Shower Pan Installation in Sheridan

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Sheridan

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Sheridan, WY

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. You get the full price before we start.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Sheridan, WY

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Sheridan

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. Then the valve. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Digital Shower Systems in Sheridan, WY

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. An electrician handles the outlet. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Sheridan, WY

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day.     Custom Shower System Installation in Sheridan, WY

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Installation in Sheridan

    Shower plumbing for a remodel, including valve, drain and trim, usually costs $1,200 to $3,500. A complete walk-in with tile and glass can reach $8,000 to $20,000, though the plumbing is a small part of that. One-piece units run lower.

    Shower plumbing gets buried behind tile. Mistakes like a loose drain clamp or bad slope hide until water damage appears in a ceiling or floor. Repairs mean demolition.

    Our workmanship warranty typically runs one year. Manufacturer warranties on the shower unit and fixtures also apply, ranging from one year to a lifetime depending on the brand and the part. We go over it before work starts.

    Center drains and linear drains cover most showers. Center drains need four-way slope, and linear drains allow a single slope that works well with big tile. We help you choose.

    Small bathrooms are where shower choice matters most. A 32-inch corner unit, a neo-angle stall or a custom tiled shower with a sliding door can fit spaces that a standard tub never could. Measurements decide it.

    Mistakes in shower plumbing end up hidden in walls and floors. We recommend a licensed plumber, especially when permits and inspections are involved.

    Large rain heads and multiple outlets use more hot water. We look at the heater's size and recovery rate and tell you if it needs an upgrade.
