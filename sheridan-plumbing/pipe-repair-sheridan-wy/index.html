    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Sheridan

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, and we replace the worst sections or the whole run with PEX or copper and dielectric fittings at every transition. You will notice the flow.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Sheridan

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Sheridan

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. Repairs are possible. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, which is why we use dielectric transitions and lay out a replacement plan for the rest. You decide the timing.

Usually a quick fix.
