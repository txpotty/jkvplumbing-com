    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Sheridan

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.
