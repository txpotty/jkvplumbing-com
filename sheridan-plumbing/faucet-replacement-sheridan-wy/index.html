    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Sheridan

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first.     Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Sheridan, WY

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. The mounting nuts sit behind the bowl in a space about the width of a hand, and after twenty years they are usually crusted to the shank.     Corroded Faucet Replacement in Sheridan

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. Replacement is the only fix. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.
