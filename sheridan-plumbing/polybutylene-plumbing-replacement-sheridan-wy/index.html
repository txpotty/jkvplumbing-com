    Why Polybutylene Fails in Sheridan

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Many rural homes in Wyoming draw from private wells, and well water can carry sulfate, iron or other minerals that affect taste and fixtures. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
