    Why Choose JKV Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Torrington, WY?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run. Ignoring them causes kinks, sags and strained fittings, and it can cost you the manufacturer warranty that should have backed the pipe.

    • Freeze Tolerance, PEX can swell when water freezes and usually holds where copper would crack.
    • Quieter Lines, PEX takes the edge off water hammer and carries less rushing sound than copper.
    • Chlorine Rated, Code-listed PEX is tested for chlorinated city water at hot-water temperatures.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Torrington

    Your service line runs from the meter or well to the house, usually 18 to 36 inches down.     PEX for Remodels & Additions in Torrington, WY

    Leak Repair with PEX in Torrington, WY

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Torrington, WY

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Replacing it with PEX can make the house easier to insure and sell.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Torrington

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Torrington

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Torrington

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Torrington

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.
