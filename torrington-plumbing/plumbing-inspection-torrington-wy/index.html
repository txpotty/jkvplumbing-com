    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Torrington, WY

    Water Heater Inspections in Torrington

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Torrington

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Torrington, WY

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Torrington, WY

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Torrington, WY

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. A locator marks the depth. Expansive clay soils are common in parts of Wyoming, and their swelling and shrinking can crack foundations and shift buried pipes.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Torrington

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Torrington, WY

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Torrington

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. Water heaters, fixtures and sewer lines get recorded unit by unit, and commercial inspections can add backflow assemblies and grease trap lines, which helps owners budget for maintenance and larger repairs.
