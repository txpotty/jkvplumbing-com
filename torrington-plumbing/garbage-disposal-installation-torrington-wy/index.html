    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Torrington

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Torrington, WY

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.

    Disposal Upgrades in Torrington, WY

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request.     Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Torrington, WY

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Torrington
