    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Torrington

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Torrington, WY

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. JKV Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Replacement in Torrington, WY

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. You will notice soft water the same day.
