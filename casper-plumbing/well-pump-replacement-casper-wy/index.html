    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Casper

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off.     Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Casper, WY

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. The switch itself is a cheap part. You get to see the old part.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Casper

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. The air charge should read 2 psi under the switch cut-in.

    Dry faucets on a well-only property mean no flushing or cooking, so we treat those as same-day calls whenever the crew and the weather allow. The truck carries common 1/2 and 3/4 HP pumps. We also bring splice kits and switches.
