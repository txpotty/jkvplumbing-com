    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Casper, WY

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor.     Cartridge Faucet Repair in Casper, WY

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature.     Dripping Faucet Repair in Casper

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Casper, WY

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own.     Emergency Faucet Repair in Casper

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Casper

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.
