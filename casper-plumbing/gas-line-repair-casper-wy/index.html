    CSST Repair in Casper

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.
