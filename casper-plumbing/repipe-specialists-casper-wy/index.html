    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Casper, WY

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Casper, WY

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.

    Copper Repiping in Casper, WY

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Expect a slightly longer job. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Casper, WY

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. The numbers add up quickly.     Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Casper, WY

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned.     Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Casper

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Casper, WY

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    PEX Repiping in Casper, WY

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.
