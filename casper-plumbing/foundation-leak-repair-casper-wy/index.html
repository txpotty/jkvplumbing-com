    Water Line Reroute in Casper

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Casper

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. JKV Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Casper foundation is sound again.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Casper, WY

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. From a pit next to the foundation, the crew tunnels by hand to the pipe, a good choice when the leak is under cabinets or expensive tile. JKV Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Casper, WY repair is done.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Casper, WY

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Casper
