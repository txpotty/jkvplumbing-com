    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Casper, WY

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Casper

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
