    Copper to PEX Conversions in Casper

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up fast in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Casper, WY

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Casper, WY can benefit from PEX as much as houses can. Each unit can get its own manifold and shutoff, so a leak in one apartment shuts down one apartment instead of the whole stack. Rated walls need firestopping. Where PEX passes through a fire-rated assembly, we use listed firestop sleeves or sealant so the rating holds.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Casper

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the foundation we install a new full-port ball valve as the main shutoff.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Casper, WY

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Casper, WY

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Casper

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Casper, WY

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. We let joints set before testing.
