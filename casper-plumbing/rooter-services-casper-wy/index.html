    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Casper

    Standard drain snaking in Casper, WY usually costs $150 to $500 depending on where the clog sits and how easy it is to reach. Main line root cutting and hydro jetting can reach $400 to $1,000 or more. A camera inspection adds to the bill. We quote after we see the setup, so you know the cost before the cable goes in.

    Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. Do not wait on that one. Several fixtures acting up at once almost always points to a main line clog rather than a single trap under one sink.

    Chemicals sometimes open a small hole in a hair clog, but they will not touch roots or a grease plug. They can soften old PVC glue joints and pit cast iron over time. They also burn skin. A rooter cable removes the clog itself rather than eating a channel through it, so the line stays open far longer.

    Plan on 1 to 3 hours for a single clogged drain. Heavy main line root cutting or a long jetting run can take 3 to 6 hours. You get a time estimate first. After looking at the cleanout and hearing which drains are slow, the tech can narrow the estimate.

    Most rooter jobs include a limited guarantee for a set number of days. A repeat clog in that window means a free return visit.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Casper's older homes take the most damage. They also put our techs at risk. Let us know if you used a chemical, because that water splashes when the trap comes off.

    Sewer pipes leak a little water vapor at the joints. Roots chase that moisture to the joint and work their way inside. Inside, they thicken fast. In Casper, clay pipe with a joint every 2 to 3 feet and older cast iron with oakum joints are the most common targets.

    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. They also get stuck. A rental cable stuck in an old clay line can turn a $300 rooter call into a dig to retrieve it.
