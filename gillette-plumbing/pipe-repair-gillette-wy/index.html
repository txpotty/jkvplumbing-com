    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Gillette, WY

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Gillette

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Gillette

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, so repairs on galvanized are usually short-term and replacing the run with PEX or copper is the lasting fix. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Gillette

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Usually a quick fix.
