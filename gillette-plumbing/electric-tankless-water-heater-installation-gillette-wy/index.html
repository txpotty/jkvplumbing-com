    Electric Tankless Installation Cost in Gillette, WY

    Point-of-use electric tankless costs far less than a whole-home system. Whole-home installs often need multiple circuits and sometimes a panel upgrade, and that electrical work can cost as much as the unit itself. We itemize the quote. We also compare operating costs with a tank.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Gillette

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Summer water helps. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.

    Rheem Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Gillette

    Rheem's RTEX line covers most of the whole-home electric tankless units we install. The RTEX-18 runs on two 40 amp breakers, while the RTEX-24 and RTEX-27 need three, so we match the model to your panel before ordering. They mount flat on the wall. We connect water, set temperature and test for leaks.

    Electric Tankless Replacement and Upgrades in Gillette

    Older electric tankless units start to struggle when elements burn out or flow sensors fail. Newer units bring better flow control, and we inspect wiring and plumbing during the swap. Error codes tell a story. Performance comes back.
