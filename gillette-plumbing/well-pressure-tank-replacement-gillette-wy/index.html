    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Gillette

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, so a torn bladder or rusted seam means a new tank, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Gillette, WY

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.
