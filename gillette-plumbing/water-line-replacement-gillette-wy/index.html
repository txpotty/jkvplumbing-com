    PEX Water Line Installation in Gillette, WY

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Homeowners here use heat tape, pipe insulation and frost-free hose bibs to keep lines from freezing in garages and crawlspaces. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Gillette, WY

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Gillette

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Gillette

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.
