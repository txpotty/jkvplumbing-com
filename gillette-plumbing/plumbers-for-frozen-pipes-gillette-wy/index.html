    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Gillette

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawlspaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens. Those are the first to freeze.     Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Gillette, WY

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in Gillette

    Pipes under a slab rarely freeze, but the lines where they rise out of the slab at an exterior wall can. The riser is where trouble starts. Acoustic and thermal tools find slab-area leaks without breaking up the floor first.

    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Gillette

    A faucet that trickles or goes dry on a cold morning points to ice somewhere in its supply line. We trace the line back with a thermal camera to find the cold spot. We thaw it with the faucet open so melting water can escape.
