    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Jackson, WY

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Jackson, WY

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, so we dig and reset the pipe on a proper bed at a proper fall. Bellies disappear.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Jackson

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Jackson

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Jackson

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Jackson, WY

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Without one, the plumber has to pull a toilet or go through the roof vent. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Jackson, WY

    Most house laterals run 30 to 100 feet. Some are two feet down, some are eight. We measure both before quoting, check whether the run passes under a driveway, porch or tree, and plan the method around what sits on top of the pipe, so the estimate you sign is the price you pay.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Jackson, WY

    Expansive clay soils are common in parts of Wyoming, and their swelling and shrinking can crack foundations and shift buried pipes. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, which can lead to uneven settling of the slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and tamp each layer of fill. Slow work near the house.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Jackson

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.
