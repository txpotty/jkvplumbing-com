    Main Water Line Repair in Jackson

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial.     Water Pressure Restoration in Jackson

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first.     Copper Water Line Repair in Jackson

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside.
