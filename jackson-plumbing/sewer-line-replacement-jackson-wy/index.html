    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Jackson, WY

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rocky ground slows the dig. We price that in after the locate shows depth. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Jackson, WY

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. If the camera shows a single offset or a cracked section and the rest of the line is round and clean, we dig one small hole at the marked depth and replace that piece with PVC and shielded couplings. That costs much less. We only suggest it when the footage backs it up.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Jackson, WY

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind. Lining is the other trenchless route. A resin-soaked tube is pulled into the cleaned host pipe and cured in place, which leaves a new pipe inside the old one and works well when the original still holds its round shape.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Jackson

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. A camera that dead-ends at broken clay or squashed fiber pipe means we have to dig, clean out the debris and replace that run. Being in the trench shows us what the surrounding soil is doing. Sometimes we find a washout or a root mat that explains why the pipe failed in the first place.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Jackson, WY

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Jackson, WY need lateral work too. Bigger flow and heavier grease mean careful sizing, extra cleanouts for jetting and attention to the grease interceptor tie-in. Some jobs need traffic control. We schedule around tenants and posted hours.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Jackson, WY
