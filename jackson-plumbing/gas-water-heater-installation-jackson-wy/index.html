    Propane Water Heater Installation in Jackson

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We call the propane company when needed. We test all fittings.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Jackson, WY

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Jackson, WY

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. JKV Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Draft is checked last.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Jackson

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Jackson, WY

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Jackson

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. Their Defender Safety System uses a sealed combustion chamber and a flame arrestor, so we follow their manual on clearances and the combustion air screen at the bottom of the tank. They are built heavy. Code-approved installs every time.

    Cost-Effective Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Jackson, WY

    Upgrading an old 0.55 UEF gas tank to a newer model can cut gas use noticeably. You get a side-by-side of efficiency, recovery and estimated yearly cost before you choose. Heavy users save most. Upkeep tips come with it.
