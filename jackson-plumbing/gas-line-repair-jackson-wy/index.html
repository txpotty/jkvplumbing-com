    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Jackson

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Basements and crawlspaces are both common in Wyoming homes, and both benefit from protection from freezing and groundwater. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Jackson, WY

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Jackson, WY

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Jackson

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Jackson, WY

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Jackson

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Repair in Jackson

    Look for dead or yellow grass in a line across the yard, bubbling in wet soil, a hissing sound or a rising gas bill. Any of those can point to a buried leak.
