    How We Set New Sinks in Jackson, WY

    Undermount Sink Installation in Jackson

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Jackson

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Jackson, WY

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Jackson, WY

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Jackson

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. We install stainless sinks with commercial faucets and basket strainers, mount them to carry a full bowl of water plus a stack of dishes, and add a hand sink where your inspector wants one. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Jackson, WY

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
