    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Riverton, WY

    Vessel Sink Installation in Riverton, WY

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Riverton

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Riverton, WY

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Riverton

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Riverton, WY

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Riverton, WY

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. The faucet holes have to match the faucet you picked, whether that is a single hole, a 4 inch centerset or an 8 inch widespread, and the overflow has to line up with the pop-up drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Riverton

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
