    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Riverton, WY

    Humming Disposal Repair in Riverton

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Riverton

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Most smells start at the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Riverton

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Riverton

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Riverton, WY

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.
