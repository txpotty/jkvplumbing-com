    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Riverton typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Pipe on an open ceiling is the cheap case.     Yes, every repair carries a written workmanship warranty. Sump pumps and parts add the manufacturer's warranty. Seepage warranty terms depend on the repair method, and we spell them out before you sign.

    Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne cover most of our installs because they are durable and parts are easy to get. We pick cast iron models for primary pumps. Battery or water-powered backups go in where power outages are common, plus a high-water alarm.

    Plumbing leaks show up no matter the weather. Seepage comes and goes with rain. Turn off every fixture and watch the water meter, and if it still moves, the leak is in the pressurized lines, while water that only appears a few hours after a storm is almost always coming from outside.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing is broader. It manages groundwater around the entire foundation with drains, membranes and a sump, and it makes sense when water enters along many points rather than one obvious spot that a single repair can fix.

    Small repairs usually do not need a permit. Larger work is different. Stack replacement and new backwater valves often need one, and we handle the permit and inspection.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. Those deposits are efflorescence, left behind when water evaporates.
