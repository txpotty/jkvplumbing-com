    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Riverton

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are common in parts of Wyoming, and their swelling and shrinking can crack foundations and shift buried pipes. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Riverton

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Riverton, WY

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Riverton, WY

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Riverton, WY. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. The permit is on us. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Riverton, WY

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Riverton

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.
