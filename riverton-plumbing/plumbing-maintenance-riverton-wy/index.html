    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Riverton, WY

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Riverton

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. We check the brine level, clean the injector screen, inspect the resin bed for fouling and confirm the regeneration cycle starts and finishes, then test the treated water hardness at a faucet. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Riverton

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. Flushing clears it. We descale tankless units through their service valves. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Riverton

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Riverton

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. A yearly camera look and a cleaning on known problem lines, especially near big trees, keeps grease and roots from building to the point where the main backs up during a holiday. We jet greasy lines. Root-prone lines go on a schedule.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Riverton

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Yearly tests are often required, and our certified testers check each valve and file results. We repair failures. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Late tests can mean fines.
