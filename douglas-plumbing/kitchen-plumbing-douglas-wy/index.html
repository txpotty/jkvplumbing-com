    Grease Trap Installation in Douglas, WY

    Under-sink grease traps work for small kitchens, while full restaurants often need an in-ground interceptor sized to the local sewer authority's rules.     InSinkErator Models We Service in Douglas

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which matters in an open kitchen, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. Common models are on the truck. Older InSinkErator units with a good motor can usually be repaired.

    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Douglas

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and either an air gap at the sink or a high loop under the counter, depending on local code. Ice makers need a cold line. We run braided stainless or copper to the fridge with a quarter-turn shutoff, never the thin plastic tubing that splits behind the fridge.

    Disposal Replacement in Douglas, WY

    Disposals last about eight to twelve years. When the housing starts weeping from the bottom, it is done. We drop the old unit, check the mounting ring, install the new one and reconnect the dishwasher hose with a new clamp so it does not leak at the inlet. The old unit leaves with us.

    Burst Supply Lines in Douglas, WY

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put gallons per minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. Both supply lines get replaced. The second line is just as old as the one that failed.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Douglas, WY

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds build up in the trap and branch line over months, not days.
