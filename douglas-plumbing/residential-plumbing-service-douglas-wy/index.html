    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Douglas, WY

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Older homes may still have galvanized supply lines that narrow with rust and scale, causing low pressure and discolored water. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Douglas, WY

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. Then we light it and check the flame.
