    Slab Leak Repiping in Douglas

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Expansive clay soils are common in parts of Wyoming, and their swelling and shrinking can crack foundations and shift buried pipes. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common.
