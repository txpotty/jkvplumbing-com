    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Douglas, WY

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one.     Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Douglas

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Douglas

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.
