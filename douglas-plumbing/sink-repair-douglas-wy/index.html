    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Douglas, WY

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. A trap or stopper costs a fraction of a new sink, and replacement only makes sense when the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain body has rusted into the basin. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Douglas, WY

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Douglas, WY

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Douglas, WY

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Douglas, WY

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Douglas

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Douglas, WY

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Douglas

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. The overflow channel gets flushed too. That slot smells when it fills with slime.
