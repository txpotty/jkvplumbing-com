    Drain Field Line Jetting in Evanston

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping.     Septic Tee Repair in Evanston

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Evanston

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. We measure sludge and scum on every maintenance visit, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Evanston, WY

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days.     Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Evanston

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.
