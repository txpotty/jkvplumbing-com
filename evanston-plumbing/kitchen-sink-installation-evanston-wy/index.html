    Composite Sink Installation in Evanston

    Granite and quartz composite sinks resist scratches on the surface but can chip at the drain hole if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first.     Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Evanston, WY

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks each have their own health code rules.     Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Evanston

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Once the bowl is mounted, the space underneath is hard to reach, so this is a job worth doing while the sink is still on the floor.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Evanston, WY

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Evanston, WY

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.
