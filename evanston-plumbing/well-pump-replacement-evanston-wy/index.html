    What We Fix on Well Systems in Evanston, WY

    Jet Pump Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Evanston

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Evanston

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Evanston

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Parts and control boxes are easy to find. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Evanston, WY

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Evanston

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. They get by with a much smaller tank because the drive matches pump speed to demand. The soft start is easier on the motor. The upfront cost is higher.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Evanston, WY

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.
