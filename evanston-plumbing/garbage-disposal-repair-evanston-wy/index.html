    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Evanston, WY

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Evanston, WY

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Evanston

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Evanston

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Evanston

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Evanston, WY

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliances. Scale gives that food something to stick to. Cleaning the guard and chamber, checking the drain and swapping a torn guard often solves a smell that lemon peels never fixed.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Evanston, WY

    A disposal repair that costs half of a new unit rarely makes sense, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Evanston

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Evanston, WY

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.
