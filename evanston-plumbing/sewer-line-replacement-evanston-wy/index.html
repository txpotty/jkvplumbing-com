    What Sewer Replacement Costs in Evanston, WY

    Four things set the price: length, depth, access and method. Open trench through an open lawn is usually the cheapest per foot, while bursting costs more per foot but can save thousands in concrete and landscaping repair. Hardscape flips the numbers. We write up every workable option with its own total after the camera run.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Evanston

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Evanston, WY

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. A typical burst takes one working day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Evanston

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time. A collapse means we put in the permit and utility locates right away. JKV Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. That keeps one toilet and the kitchen sink usable while the dig gets scheduled.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Evanston

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Evanston

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A transmitter in the head lets us paint the spot and depth above ground. The footage tells us whether one section or the whole line needs to go.
