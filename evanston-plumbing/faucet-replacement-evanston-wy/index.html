    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Evanston

    Labor for a faucet replacement usually runs $150 to $350. With a solid mid-range faucet included, most jobs total somewhere between $300 and $750, depending on the model and the condition of the stops. High-end and touchless models can top $800.

    Worn cartridges and O-rings cause most leaks. A water softener or filtration system is a common upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. Scale hardens seals and scratches cartridge faces, and pressure above about 80 psi wears them faster. Corrosion on the body is the other cause.

    Drips that come back after a repair are the clearest sign. Visible corrosion on the body, handles that grind or stick, a base that wobbles on the deck and low flow that cleaning the aerator does not fix all point toward replacement. Sometimes it is just looks.

    We work on every major brand. Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister make up most of the faucets we replace, and we know their mounting hardware and cartridges by sight. We handle off-brand models as well.

    Most swaps take one to three hours. Drilling a new hole in stone, replacing corroded stubs or cutting out a fused faucet adds time, sometimes most of a morning. We stay out of your way.
