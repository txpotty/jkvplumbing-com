    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Evanston

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Evanston, WY

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Evanston, WY

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.
