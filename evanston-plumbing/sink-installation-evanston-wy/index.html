    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Evanston

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Evanston, WY

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Evanston, WY

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Evanston

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. A same-size drop-in swap usually takes a morning, and a deeper bowl may only need a new trap. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Evanston, WY

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Evanston

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Evanston

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
