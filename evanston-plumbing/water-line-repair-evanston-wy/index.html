    Underground Leak Detection in Evanston, WY

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Evanston

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Evanston
