    Anode Rod Checks in Evanston

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Evanston

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in Evanston

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. Model details matter. The truck carries common parts.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Evanston

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Evanston

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Many rural homes in Wyoming draw from private wells, and well water can carry sulfate, iron or other minerals that affect taste and fixtures. The tank gets drained until it runs clear, then refilled, and the anode, relief valve and connections get checked. Takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Evanston, WY

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Evanston, WY

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.
