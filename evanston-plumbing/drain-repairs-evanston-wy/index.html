    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Evanston may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. We pull it when one applies.

    Water coming up somewhere other than where you are draining. It is the clearest sign. If the tub fills when the washer drains, or the toilet bubbles when the sink runs, the trouble is downstream of both fixtures, often in the main line. We check it on video.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair changes the pipe itself.
