    Tile Shower Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Grout cracks and failed waterproofing are how tile showers die. Our job is the valve, drain and pan, and we coordinate with the tile installer on the waterproofing membrane. Many installers use Kerdi. The membrane covers the backer board.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Evanston

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. The tub comes out, the drain is adjusted and the valve goes in at the right height with grab bar blocking and a handheld. No tall curb. It is one of the safest changes you can make.

    Accessible Shower Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. The valve goes within reach of a seat, blocking goes in for grab bars and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Entries stay wide. Seats can be built in or fold-down.

    Custom Shower Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Built-in benches and niches change the plumbing plan. A bench affects where the valve and handheld should go, and a niche has to stay clear of supply lines, so we lay out the plumbing with your contractor before the walls are framed or furred. More outlets need larger supplies. Line size follows your pressure reading.

    Leaking Shower Pan Replacement in Evanston, WY

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Whether it is a prefab base with a crack or a tiled pan with a failed liner, we tear it out, replace any rotted subfloor and install a new pan that gets flood-tested for a full day. We replace the drain as well. It ends the cycle.

    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Evanston

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. We open the wall from the back side when we can, remove the old valve and install a pressure-balance or thermostatic valve, then cover the old opening with an oversized trim plate. Tile often stays put. It is a big safety upgrade.

    Rental and Hotel Shower Replacement in Evanston

    Rental showers need to be durable and easy to fix. We use commercial parts and the same cartridge in every unit, which keeps repairs fast. We work around tenants. Turnovers are a good time to do it.

    Shower Unit Replacement in Evanston

    Cracked fiberglass units leak at the base first. Cracks tend to start where the base was not supported, and each shower flexes it until water slips through to the subfloor. Out comes the old one. Then we check the subfloor and set the new base on a full mortar bed.
