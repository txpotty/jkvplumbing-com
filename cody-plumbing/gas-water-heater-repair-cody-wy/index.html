    Propane Water Heater Repair in Cody

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. A propane tank needs its own orifice and a valve set for roughly 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. We check pressure with a manometer. A bad conversion gets corrected with the right parts.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Cody

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Cody, WY

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. We pull the burner, brush the ports and blow out the orifice.
