    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Cody

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Cody

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it.     Slab Leak Reroutes in Cody

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.
