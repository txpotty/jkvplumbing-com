    Solar Well Pump Systems in Cody, WY

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. The storage tank does the job a battery would. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Cody

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Cody

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Well Pump Sizing in Cody

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Cody, WY

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Cody, WY

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. Those get tested first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Cody, WY

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Cody

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Parts and control boxes are easy to find. We match the motor to the pump end and the wire size to the run length.
