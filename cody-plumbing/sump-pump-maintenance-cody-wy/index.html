    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Cody, WY

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats hang up once minerals coat the rod. For narrow basins where a float keeps catching, an electronic switch with no moving float is a good swap that ends the problem.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Cody

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Cody

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Cody

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Cody

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Cody, WY

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Cody

    Spring snowmelt can raise creeks and groundwater in Wyoming, sometimes pushing water into basements and crawlspaces. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Cody, WY

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.
