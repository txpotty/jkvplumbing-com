    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Cody

    A floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open.     Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Cody

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.

    Sump Pit and Pump Installation in Cody

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. We use a cast iron pump in most homes and a 1-1/2 inch discharge with a check valve. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places. It has to discharge outside, far enough from the foundation that it does not cycle back into the pit, often ten feet or more.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Cody

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Cody, WY

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale from the inside out. We cut out cracked cast iron and splice in new pipe with shielded no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Cody, WY

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Cody, WY
