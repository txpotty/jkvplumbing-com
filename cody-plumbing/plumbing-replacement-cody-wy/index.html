    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Cody

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. Replacing the tank stops it. The pressure switch gets checked too.

    A dead sump pump on a wet night means water on the floor, and a dead well pump means no water at all.
