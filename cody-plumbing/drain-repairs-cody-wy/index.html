    Recurring Clog Repair in Cody

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. The fix goes where the clog starts.

    Sewer Drain Line Repair in Cody

    Every drain in the house empties into one building drain. One blockage, every fixture. Cracks, offsets and collapses in that line cause whole-house backups, so we use a liner when the pipe allows it and a targeted excavation when it does not. We decide after seeing the footage.

    Main Line Drain Repair in Cody

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. Basement and first-floor drains go first.     Burst Drain Line Repair in Cody, WY

    Inside a wall, a broken drain line can soak flooring fast. Stop running water to it. When you call with an active leak, we move the visit up, isolate the damaged section and replace the broken pipe on the first visit in most cases. You will hear if anything needs drying out.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Cody, WY

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Undersized drains spill over. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, then refill or repair floor drain traps so they hold water and block sewer gas. We fix primers that keep those seals full.
