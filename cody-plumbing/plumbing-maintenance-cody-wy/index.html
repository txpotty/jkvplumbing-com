    Sump Pump Testing in Cody, WY

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Spring snowmelt can raise creeks and groundwater in Wyoming, sometimes pushing water into basements and crawlspaces. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Cody

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Cody

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Resin cleaner helps. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Cody, WY

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Cody

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Hard, mineral-heavy water is common across Wyoming, and scale builds up fast in water heaters, faucets and appliances. The tank gets flushed, the anode rod gets checked, the relief valve gets tested and the connections get inspected. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Cody

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Most failures give warning. A yearly tune-up catches the warning signs.
