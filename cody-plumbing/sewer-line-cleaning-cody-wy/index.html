    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Rentals see more clogs from wipes and grease. Turnover adds to it. We offer scheduled cleanings for landlords and property managers, and for HOAs we clean shared lines and document each building so the budget can be planned, coordinating access with tenants.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Cody, WY

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Digging costs far more. A sound pipe is worth maintaining, while a collapsed or sagging one may need repair, and the camera tells us which, and you see both prices.

    Commercial Sewer Line Cleaning in Cody, WY

    Commercial laterals see heavy use. Restaurants fight grease daily. Our larger cables and jetters handle commercial lines, and we work around your hours so customers are not affected.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Cody, WY

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. High-pressure water strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes them toward the street, and with the right settings it is safe for PVC and older clay alike.

    Restaurant and Shop Sewer Lines in Cody, WY

    Commercial lines clog in their own ways. Grease in kitchens. Restaurants battle heavy grease while other businesses see paper, debris or chemical waste, and our heavy-duty equipment handles larger pipe and more severe blockages, working quickly to limit downtime.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Cody

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Cody, WY

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Gurgling is another. Sewer odors, slow drains in several rooms and water rising in the tub when the toilet flushes are all warning signs.
