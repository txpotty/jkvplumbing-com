    CSST Repair in Cody

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Cody

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Cody building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspection usually happens within a few days. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Cody

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Cody, WY

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. Utilities get marked first. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Cody, WY

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Nails, rodents and lightning can all damage CSST, and older yellow-jacket tubing must be bonded to the home's grounding system to lower arc risk. Bonding is a short job. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Drought is a recurring concern in Wyoming, and dry, shrinking soil can shift foundations and buried lines.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Cody, WY

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Cody, WY

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.
