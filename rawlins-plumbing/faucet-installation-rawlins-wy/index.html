    How We Install Faucets in Rawlins, WY

    Vessel Sink Faucets in Rawlins, WY

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. A tall single-hole faucet or a wall-mount spout works, and we pick the height so the stream lands near the drain instead of the bowl rim. Reach matters as much as height. A short spout means knuckles against the bowl edge.

    Pot Filler Installation in Rawlins

    A pot filler needs its own cold line. We tap a cold supply, run it up the wall behind the range and set a shutoff where you can reach it, which code and common sense both want. The arm folds flat when not in use. The line is tested under pressure before anyone tiles over it.

    Commercial and ADA Faucets in Rawlins, WY

    ADA rules set faucet reach and handle force. Handles have to work with one hand and no tight grip, which is why lever and sensor faucets dominate commercial restrooms. We schedule around your hours. Fixtures go in with the spec sheet left for the inspector.

    Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in Rawlins

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. We mount wall or deck models with an integral vacuum breaker, since a hose left in a sink full of soapy water is a textbook backflow risk. Supplies are strapped so they cannot rub.

    Commercial-Style Kitchen Faucets in Rawlins

    Commercial-style spring spout faucets are tall. Many spring spouts stand over 20 inches, so we measure up to the window sill or cabinet before you buy. Any extra sink hole can hold a soap pump or side sprayer. We set the hose weight where the spout docks without slapping the cabinet.

    Utility Sink Faucet Installation in Rawlins, WY

    Utility tubs see hot water and harsh cleaners. A utility faucet needs a brass body and a firm mount, because people yank hoses off it, and a loose faucet cracks its own supply connection. The spout gets a vacuum breaker. We check these joints twice, since a garage tub can drip for months before anyone notices.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Rawlins, WY

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. We bolt the wall bracket into solid backing and fit check valves on both supplies, since a pre-rinse unit gets pulled hard all shift. Hand sinks get wrist blades or sensors. We work after close so the line keeps running.

    Garage and Workshop Faucets in Rawlins

    Workshop and garage sinks get abused. These need metal handles and a solid anchor, since a yanked hose will twist a loose faucet off its supply lines. Flow is checked at full open. A slow tub drain gets its trap cleared at the same visit.
