    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Rawlins

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger. A scrub pad and some vinegar clean most seats, and if it is pitted we install a seat repair kit or a new flush valve. Then we set the float so the water stops about an inch below the overflow.

    Toilet Brands We Service in Rawlins

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. The wrong flapper will leak. We read the model number cast inside the tank wall and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Rawlins

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We swap them for an adjustable fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. The tank gets noticeably quieter.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Rawlins, WY

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. We carry adapters for some of them. For the rest, we will tell you if parts are available or if replacement is the honest answer.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Rawlins

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Running Toilet Repair in Rawlins

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is usually leaking past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast. We check the chain, the float height, the flapper and the seat before touching a part, then replace only what failed. Most running toilets are quiet again in about thirty minutes.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Rawlins

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. The fix is to tighten or rebuild the carrier, then reseal the bowl. That one needs care. Leave a loose wall-hung bowl alone long enough and the china can crack at the mounting holes.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Rawlins, WY

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way. A gurgle from a nearby sink or tub when you flush points to a vent problem. We clear the trap with an auger and treat the jets with a descaler. Some early 1.6 gallon toilets from the 1990s just flush poorly, and we will say so.
