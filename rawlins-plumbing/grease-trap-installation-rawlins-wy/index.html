    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Rawlins

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. The tank holds water for roughly half an hour, which gives grease time to rise and food solids time to drop. Sizing comes from your fixture units and the retention time your sewer authority wants, and we check that number with them before ordering a tank. No moving parts to fail.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Rawlins

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. The purchase price is higher than a passive trap. They also need a dedicated electrical circuit, which we coordinate with your electrician before the unit arrives. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Rawlins, WY

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.
