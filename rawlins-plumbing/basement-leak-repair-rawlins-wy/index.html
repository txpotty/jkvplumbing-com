    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Rawlins

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Rawlins typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap. Pipe behind finished drywall or under the slab adds the cost of opening and patching, and a cast iron stack replacement can run higher than the range above if it goes through more than one floor.

    Plumbing leaks show up no matter the weather. Seepage comes and goes with rain. Turn off every fixture and watch the water meter, and if it still moves, the leak is in the pressurized lines, while water that only appears a few hours after a storm is almost always coming from outside.

    Yes, every repair carries a written workmanship warranty. Sump pumps and parts add the manufacturer's warranty. Coverage for seepage work depends on the method, since an interior drain system can carry a long warranty while a crack injection is warranted against leaking at that crack, and we write the terms out before you approve.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. That white crust is mineral left after water dries. A rising water bill or rust at the water heater base points toward a plumbing leak. Smell often comes first.

    Workmanship on pipe repairs is warranted for the term on your invoice. Pumps come with the manufacturer's warranty. Seepage repairs carry terms that match the method, and if water returns at the same spot during the warranty period we come back, retest it, and fix it without charging for the labor.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Bigger jobs often do. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.

    A pipe or fitting repair usually takes an hour or two. Injecting a crack is a half-day job. Sump pit installation takes most of a day, and an interior perimeter drain system takes two to five days depending on wall length and how much finished space has to be cleared first.
