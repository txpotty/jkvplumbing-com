    Steam Shower Plumbing in Rawlins

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit.     Curbless Shower Installation in Rawlins

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Rawlins

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on.
