    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Rawlins

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Rawlins, WY

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Rawlins

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Basements and crawlspaces are both common in Wyoming homes, and both benefit from protection from freezing and groundwater. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Rawlins, WY

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. Old galvanized gets removed or abandoned and replaced with PEX or copper, and most homes see better pressure and clearer water right away.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Rawlins

    Older commercial buildings in Rawlins often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code.     Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Rawlins, WY

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more.
