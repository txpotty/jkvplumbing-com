    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Rawlins, WY

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. We take the lid off, remove grease and solids, scrape the baffles, check the flow control fitting on the inlet, and replace the lid gasket if it is flattened or torn. Most odor from these units comes from a bad gasket.

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in Rawlins, WY

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Rawlins, WY

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Preventive Maintenance Programs in Rawlins, WY

    A maintenance plan is just a pumping interval plus someone who tracks it. We keep the calendar so your manager does not have to.     Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Rawlins

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. Both get checked every visit. Most smells get fixed that visit.
