    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Rawlins, WY

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Rawlins, WY

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Rawlins, WY

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Rawlins

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.
