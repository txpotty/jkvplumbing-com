    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Powell, WY

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Powell

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Powell

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Powell

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. The pump gets reprimed and resealed, and the foot valve and suction line get checked, because a single air leak will undo the work. Everything is above ground.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Powell

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Powell, WY well.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Powell

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Powell, WY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Drought is a recurring concern in Wyoming, and dry, shrinking soil can shift foundations and buried lines. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Powell, WY

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.
