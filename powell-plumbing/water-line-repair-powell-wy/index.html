    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Powell

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Powell, WY

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. A water softener or filtration system is a popular upgrade for Wyoming homeowners dealing with scale and staining. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Powell, WY

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Powell, WY

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patching that kind of pipe tends to buy months, not years. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, usually in one day with a trench or a trenchless pull. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Powell, WY

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. JKV Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Powell

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Powell

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Powell, WY

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.
