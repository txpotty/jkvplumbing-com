    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Powell

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Powell, WY

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Powell

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Backup Pump Options in Powell, WY

    Spring snowmelt can raise creeks and groundwater in Wyoming, sometimes pushing water into basements and crawlspaces. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Water-powered backups use city water pressure and need no battery, but they only make sense with strong municipal pressure and an approved backflow preventer on the line. They will not work on a well.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Powell, WY

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. We move those calls to the front of the line.     Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Powell, WY

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in.     Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Powell

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Powell

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. Out comes the old pump, out comes the muck at the bottom of the basin, and the new one goes in on a brick to stay clear of silt. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.
