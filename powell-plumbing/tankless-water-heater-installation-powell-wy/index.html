    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Powell, WY

    Upfront, a tankless install in Powell, WY costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows.     Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Powell

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. It shortens the list quickly.
