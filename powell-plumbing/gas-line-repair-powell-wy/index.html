    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Powell

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Powell, WY

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Powell, WY

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.
