    Corroded Drain Replacement in Powell, WY

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Powell, WY

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked arm or rusted stub drips down the back panel, out of sight, until the wood swells or smells. The bad section gets cut back. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Powell

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Powell

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.
