    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Powell, WY

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Powell

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Powell

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Powell

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Powell, WY

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Tankless jobs cost more mostly because of gas line and vent work, while attic installs, moves and code updates add labor to any type. Permits are included. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Powell, WY

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour.     Expansion Tank Installation in Powell, WY

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
