    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Powell, WY

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Powell

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Powell

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Powell

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Powell, WY

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Powell, WY

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Powell, WY

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Powell, WY

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. A WaterSense model uses 1.28 gallons or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Check with your water provider about rebates.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Powell

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.
