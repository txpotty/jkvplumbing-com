    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Powell

    Many rural homes in Wyoming draw from private wells, and well water can carry sulfate, iron or other minerals that affect taste and fixtures. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.
